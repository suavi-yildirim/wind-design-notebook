### Why so tall?
Wind blows stronger and steadier with height. Because power grows with the cube of wind speed, a few more metres of hub height bring noticeably more yield. At the top you see the verdict for a 5 MW turbine at 90 m – and which check is currently setting the limit.

<!-- id: tour.2 -->
### Why does the tower vibrate?
The rotor pushes the tower once per revolution (1P) and three times when a blade passes (3P). The natural frequency must lie in between. In the Campbell diagram that is the horizontal line f₁ between the two sloped bands.

<!-- id: tour.3 -->
### Why doesn't it fit on the road?
Now a 7 MW turbine at 160 m as a pure steel tower. To be stiff enough it needs a 6 m diameter at the base – but only about 4.30 m fits under motorway bridges. Transport governs and the verdict turns red.
